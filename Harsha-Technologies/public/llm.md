# Harsha Technologies

Harsha Technologies is a printer repair and office printer maintenance service owned by Pothugunta Naresh and based in Kukatpally, Hyderabad.

The business provides doorstep printer repair for homes and offices, with support for HP, Canon, Epson, Brother, Samsung, Xerox, Ricoh, Kyocera, Panasonic, Zebra and other major printer brands.

Primary services include printer repair, HP printer repair, paper jam repair, printer offline troubleshooting, WiFi printer setup, driver installation, laser printer repair, inkjet printer repair, scanner support, toner and cartridge help, and office printer AMC.

Commercial customers include offices, IT companies, schools, colleges, clinics, hospitals, co-working spaces, Xerox centers, retail outlets and multi-location businesses.

Primary service areas include Kukatpally, KPHB, Miyapur, Nizampet, Moosapet, JNTU, Bachupally, Pragathi Nagar, Madhapur, HITEC City, Gachibowli, Ameerpet, Erragadda, Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills and Chandanagar.
